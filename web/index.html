RoadReport Log in

Saw a road accident in Bulacan? Report it here.

Send the place, time and photos in a few minutes. Traffic authorities review every report, and you can follow its status until the case is closed.

Report an accident Track my report

In an emergency, call 911 first. This system is for reporting, not for dispatching help.

  1. Describe what happened

    Date, time, type of accident, vehicles and injuries.

  2. Pin the location

    Use your phone's GPS or tap the spot on the map.

  3. Add photos and send

    You get a report ID right away, and a notice each time the status changes.

Also available as an Android app. Traffic officers and administrators log in here too.
